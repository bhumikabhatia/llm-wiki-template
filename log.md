# Wiki Log

> Chronological record of all wiki actions. Append-only.
> Format: `## [YYYY-MM-DD] action | subject`
> Actions: ingest, update, query, lint, create, archive, delete

Each entry lists what was created or updated, e.g.:

```
## [YYYY-MM-DD] ingest | weekly-sync-notes

- Source: raw/transcripts/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-sync.md
- Files created: decisions/adr-use-queue-for-retries.md
- Files updated: entities/systems/payments-service.md, index.md (page count 12→13)
- Notes: contradicts earlier claim in concepts/retry-policy.md; flagged contested
```

Newest entries go at the top or bottom — pick one and stay consistent.

Delete this explainer once you have your first real entry.
